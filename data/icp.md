# Ideal Customer Profile

Product: a developer platform that lets engineering teams ship AI agents which
act in third-party apps (auth, tool integrations, execution, observability).

## Strong fit
- B2B software, SaaS, fintech, devtools, or AI-native companies.
- 20–2,000 employees. Funded startups and scale-ups are the sweet spot.
- Building or clearly planning AI agents/assistants/copilots, LLM features, or
  workflow automation. Signals: AI on the homepage, AI/ML job posts, recent
  launches or funding announcements mentioning agents.
- Buyer persona: engineering leadership (CTO, VP/Head of Engineering, Eng
  Manager), AI/ML leads, staff+ engineers, founders, product leaders for AI features.

## Weak fit
- Agencies/consultancies (sometimes good partners, rarely direct buyers).
- Enterprises above 10k employees (long cycles; route to sales-assist, not hot).
- Non-technical roles (marketing, HR, finance) unless the company itself is a strong fit.

## Disqualifiers
- Students, personal/free-mail addresses with no identifiable company.
- Competitors building the same infrastructure.
- Disposable or invalid email addresses.

## Scoring guide
- 80–100 (hot): strong-fit company AND a technical or executive buyer AND an AI signal.
- 50–79 (warm): two of the three.
- 0–49 (cold): one or none, or any disqualifier.
